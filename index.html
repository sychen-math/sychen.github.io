About

Academic profile

I am an assistant professor in the Department of Mathematics at National Tsing Hua University. My research concerns the arithmetic of automorphic forms, with particular emphasis on periods, special values of L-functions, and their relation to Deligne's conjecture.

Research

Current themes

My work combines automorphic representation theory, cohomological methods, explicit local calculations, and arithmetic applications of period relations.

01

Automorphic periods

Rational structures, Betti–Whittaker periods, duality, and compatibility with motivic periods.

02

Special L-values

Algebraicity results for Rankin–Selberg, adjoint, Asai, triple product, and symmetric power L-functions.

03

Local representation theory

Whittaker functions, gamma factors, local zeta integrals, and explicit theta lifting.
